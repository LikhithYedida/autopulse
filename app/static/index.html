METHODOLOGY

Evidence

01 Official identity

VIN identity is decoded with NHTSA vPIC manufacturer data.

02 Source-aware evidence

Complaint, recall and crash-test evidence are loaded independently. A source outage is never interpreted as zero events.

03 Explainable signals

AutoPulse keeps complaint burden, severity, recall context and trend evidence visible instead of hiding everything behind one score.

AutoPulse is a portfolio decision-support platform. Its risk signal is not an official government safety rating. When core evidence is unavailable, AutoPulse may withhold the risk signal rather than imply a false zero. VIN-specific open recalls should always be verified directly with NHTSA. Ask Analyst explains retrieved AutoPulse evidence and does not replace official recall, inspection or history verification.
